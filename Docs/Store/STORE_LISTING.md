## Full description (4000)
Pull back, aim for the head and let the arrow fly!

Archer Arcade is a colourful archery duel game. Read the wind, bend your shot over walls, bounce it off pads and
land the perfect headshot. Every hit looks and feels great: lightning strikes from the sky, fireballs, bomb
blasts, ice and poison clouds — and your opponent looks more and more battered (cartoon style, no blood).

🏹 CAMPAIGN — THE WHISPERING FOREST
• 20 hand-made levels: duels, target practice, apple shots, rescue the fox, gauntlets and trick shots
• Crate towers with TNT, swinging targets, moving platforms, bounce pads and shields
• Mini-boss Captain Thorn and the mighty Forest Warden with rotating shields and vine walls
• Earn up to 3 stars per level

⚡ 4 ARCHERS, 8 ARROW TIPS
• Ranger, Fire Archer, Electric Archer and Bomb Archer — each with a passive and a special ability
• Fire, Electric, Split, Bomb, Heavy, Ice and Poison tips, unlocked as you play
• Upgrade your archers, pick skins and arrow trails — all with coins you earn in the game

👥 PLAY WITH A FRIEND
• 2 players on one phone: pass the phone, pick archers, best of 1, 3 or 5
• Handicap, wind and aim-preview options for fair duels

🎯 MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Quick Duel against the computer (Easy, Medium, Hard)
• Survival: how many waves can you beat?
• Daily Challenge: the same level for everyone each day, with a streak reward
• Training Range with the full aim line and no timer

🏅 22 badges with bronze, silver and gold tiers, chests, stats and more.

❤️ FREE FOREVER
• No ads. No purchases. No energy timers. No accounts.
• Works fully offline; your progress is saved only on your phone.
• English and Hinglish, light and dark theme, left-handed layout, bigger buttons, reduce motion,
  colour-blind friendly, 60/90/120 Hz.

# Archer Arcade — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 September 2026 · App: Archer Arcade (com.naveencodes.archerarcade) · Developer: NaveenCodes by
Maa Labs™_

Archer Arcade is a free, offline game. We built it so that it does not need any of your personal information.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Archer Arcade does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device data.

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising IDs.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No in-app purchases or payment details.
- The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What stays on your phone
Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, archers, badges, settings and the player name you type) is saved in the
app's private storage on your device. Only the app can read it. Uninstalling Archer Arcade, or clearing its
storage in Android settings, deletes it. Android's own device backup may include this file if you have backup
turned on; that is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

## Vibration
The app can vibrate your phone for game feedback. You can turn this off in Settings ▸ Sound ▸ Haptics.

## Children
The game has no ads, chat, purchases or data collection, so no personal information is collected from anyone,
including children.

## Changes
If a future version adds online play (planned for version 2.0), this policy will be updated before that version is
released, and the app will say what is needed and why.

## Contact
Questions: through the contact details on the Google Play listing or naveencodes.com/archer-arcade/.
